Perilune · fittings catalogue · thirty
Everything you can build
Ship stock throughout: welded tube, punched sheet, standoff feet on the deck grid, vents, latches, tie-downs. Three rules hold across the set — one scale for all thirty, everything standing on the same floor line with a centimetre rule at its left, boxes drawn in the galley plate’s oblique and anything round drawn level, so a round fitting has no heading and can be set down any way about.

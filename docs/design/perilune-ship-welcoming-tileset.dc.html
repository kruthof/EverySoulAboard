1a A ship you'd want to live in
The current decks are cold grey metal on concrete — technically a ship, emotionally a parking garage. Swapping the materials does the heavy lifting: warm plank & timber floors, navy-steel bulkheads with a warm amber trim-light, and a distinct tint + texture per room so each space feels purpose-built and homey. Lighting stays soft and practical — no sparkle, just glow from where power actually is.

Crew, drawn in ink

Two-pass figures from src/render/pawn-svg.js — one pure builder, one path list, serving the in-world pawn (16×24) and the roster chip (16×20). Every stroke is emitted twice: a paper knockout that carves the figure out of whatever it stands on, then the ink. Nobody has a colour. A soul's cid chooses their build, their topper, their personal mark and their height; their ROLE chooses the tool in their hand. The one oxblood on a person is a keepsake.

PERILUNE — item library

All 68 pieces of the warm item set, each drawn by its pure SVG builder (client/src/items/*) on the mock's warm stage. Green = FUNCTIONAL (sim DeviceKind), grey = COSMETIC (decor), blue = MATERIAL (wall/floor), tan = RESOURCE (sim ItemKind — a ground stack). amber tag = a NEW DeviceKind (not yet in the sim). Rendered live from src/items/index.js, no build step.
